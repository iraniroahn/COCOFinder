    Ethanol free (E0) petrol

    Regular petrol in India is now E20 (20% ethanol). The only petrol sold without ethanol is the 100 octane premium grade: XP100 at IndianOil, poWer 100 at HP and Speed 100 at BPCL, and only at select pumps. Speed 97 and XP95 are not ethanol free.

    What is a COCO pump?

    COCO stands for Company Owned, Company Operated. These outlets are run directly by the oil company rather than a dealer, so many drivers prefer them for consistent fuel quality and accurate measurement.

    Where the data comes from

    Pump locations come from OpenStreetMap. XP100 outlets come from IndianOil's official list. Stock changes, so call ahead before a long drive. Spot a mistake? Tell us on GitHub.
